A little about me

a peek inside how my mind wanders

I'm someone who enjoys exploring different fields, learning how things work and discovering new things along the way. I've always been drawn to questions, little details and ideas that make me look at things differently. I enjoy understanding the reasoning behind things, looking at situations from different perspectives and occasionally going down a rabbit hole simply because something caught my attention.

Things I've explored

Curiosity that turns into projects.

team project

NANOTET

A team-built, smartphone-controlled metal detection robot designed to independently navigate and locate buried metal objects. The concept combined metal detection with GPS technology and Android-based smartphone control, with the aim of making detection faster, more reliable and less dependent on human intervention, even in challenging environments.

Working on it gave me an opportunity to explore how an idea could be translated into a practical technological model through teamwork, experimentation and problem-solving.

internship

Learning through technology — IIT Bhubaneswar

My internship was an opportunity to step into areas of technology I had mostly encountered from a distance.

From experimenting with Python programming and writing algorithms to exploring MATLAB, matrix operations and 3D printing, it introduced me to different ways technology can be applied to solve problems. I also got an introduction to machine learning algorithms, artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, drone technology and the functioning of UAVs and USPRs.

One of the most enjoyable parts was exploring augmented and virtual reality through interactive games and real-time demonstrations at NTPC. What stayed with me was the sheer range of possibilities technology opens up, and how much there is still to learn.

coursework

Academic reports

Wrote detailed reports on Entrepreneurship Development and Start-ups and Fundamentals of Investment and Planning as part of B.Com coursework, researching concepts, avenues and real examples.

Experiences that shaped me

Debating has been a recurring part of my school and college experiences, beginning as early as Class IV. Over the years, I've participated in several inter-school and college-level debate competitions, usually taking part in two to three competitions each year. I've also been involved in Model United Nations and other literary and public-speaking activities.

One particularly memorable experience was participating in the Anjali International Children and Youth Festival, where I secured second position in a debate competition. What I enjoy most about debating is the process of looking at an issue from different perspectives, questioning assumptions and putting an idea into words that others can understand. It's also taught me that a good argument isn't always about proving a point; sometimes, it's about understanding one better.

Different perspectives.

You can learn something even when you don't agree with someone.
